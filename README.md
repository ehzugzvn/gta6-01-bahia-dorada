Bahía Dorada: High Water is an original open-world crime game set in a golden-hour coastal bay city, rendered in 2D canvas with pseudo-3D extruded buildings, long sunset shadows, shimmering water, palms and a beach. You play Rocco "Rayo" Ferreira, an ex-harbour pilot turned wheelman, working a season of jobs across six districts — Muelle Viejo, Playa Serena, Centro, Las Lomas, Puerto Industrial and the Isla Faro islet, reached by causeway and watched over by its lighthouse. Everything is drawn and synthesised in code: no assets, no network requests, fully playable offline in a desktop browser.

The city is alive and reactive: traffic follows the road grid, pedestrians stroll and flee, and the Dorada Patrol escalates with your wanted stars — patrol cars at one star, PIT rams at two, roadblocks at three, the FARO-1 helicopter with its sweeping searchlight plus spike strips at four, and SRT tactical vans at five — while heat decays when you break line of sight. Systems include on-foot mouse-aim shooting with a five-weapon wheel (fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle) and pickups, car theft and carjacking, vehicle damage with burning, exploding wrecks, nine staged story jobs from crew contacts, street races against three rivals, stunt ramps, a garage that sells engine/armor/paint upgrades, per-district standing and respect ranks, a cash economy, a three-person crew with Nitro, Decoy and Bribe abilities, a full HUD with a live minimap, an atlas map screen, busted and wasted screens, localStorage saves, and WebAudio synth sound with three radio stations.

Controls: WASD to move or drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter/exit or steal a car, Tab to cycle weapons, Shift to sprint (or boost with engine upgrades), Space for the handbrake, 1/2/3 for crew abilities, R to start a race at the Playa Serena start line and to change radio station, M for the atlas, Esc to pause, X to mute.

## Run it

```
node serve.ts
```

then open http://localhost:8080.
